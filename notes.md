Short rules from repeated inference failures.

---

[Good fits don't imply identifiability](/notes/good-fits/)  
A model can fit while parameters remain non-unique.

[Spectra are constraints, not fingerprints](/notes/spectra-constraints/)  
A spectrum narrows explanations; it does not automatically identify structure.

[Averaging destroys information irreversibly](/notes/averaging/)  
A mean response does not determine the distribution that produced it.

[Simulation is not deployment evidence](/notes/simulation-not-deployment/)  
A controlled model result is evidence only inside its assumptions.

[Measurement is not control](/notes/measurement-not-control/)  
A signal can be sensitive without being stable, repeatable, or actionable enough for process control.
