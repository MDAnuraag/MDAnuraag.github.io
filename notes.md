<ul class="index">
  <li><a href="/notes/good-fits/">Good fits don't imply identifiability</a><span class="d">A model can fit while parameters remain non-unique.</span></li>
  <li><a href="/notes/spectra-constraints/">Spectra are constraints, not fingerprints</a><span class="d">A spectrum narrows explanations; it does not automatically identify structure.</span></li>
  <li><a href="/notes/averaging/">Averaging destroys information irreversibly</a><span class="d">A mean response does not determine the distribution that produced it.</span></li>
  <li><a href="/notes/simulation-not-deployment/">Simulation is not deployment evidence</a><span class="d">A controlled model result is evidence only inside its assumptions.</span></li>
  <li><a href="/notes/measurement-not-control/">Measurement is not control</a><span class="d">A signal can be sensitive without being stable, repeatable, or actionable enough for process control.</span></li>
</ul>
