<div class="grid2">
  <article>
    <h3><a href="/notes/good-fits/">Good fits don't imply identifiability</a></h3>
    <p>A model can fit while parameters remain non-unique.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h3><a href="/notes/spectra-constraints/">Spectra are constraints, not fingerprints</a></h3>
    <p>A spectrum narrows explanations; it does not automatically identify structure.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h3><a href="/notes/averaging/">Averaging destroys information irreversibly</a></h3>
    <p>A mean response does not determine the distribution that produced it.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h3><a href="/notes/simulation-not-deployment/">Simulation is not deployment evidence</a></h3>
    <p>A controlled model result is evidence only inside its assumptions.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h3><a href="/notes/measurement-not-control/">Measurement is not control</a></h3>
    <p>A signal can be sensitive without being stable, repeatable, or actionable enough for process control.</p>
  </article>
</div>
