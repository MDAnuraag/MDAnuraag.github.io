## What loads from other providers

When you open a page, your browser may request resources from third parties. Those providers can see your IP address and basic request details under their own policies.

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site and keeps its own server logs.
- **Google Fonts** serves the typefaces.
- **jsDelivr** serves KaTeX for math rendering.
- **cdnjs** serves Chart.js on the interactive project pages.
- **Spotify** embeds appear on the [Elsewhere](/cv/elsewhere/) page and load only there.
