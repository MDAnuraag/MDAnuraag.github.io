Projects as evidence.

The common test:

> Can the measurement support the claim?

My current direction is fab metrology process engineering. That changes how I read these projects.

A result matters more when it can help monitor a parameter, detect drift, bound a tolerance, expose a failure mode, or support a process decision.

---

## Fab metrology and process control

### [Inverse RCWA Metrology](/work/inverse-rcwa/)

**Object:** patterned semiconductor stack  
**Signal:** broadband optical reflectance  
**Method:** inverse RCWA  
**Found:** trench depth was stable under bounds  
**Failed:** secondary geometry remained degenerate  
**Lesson:** fit quality is not identifiability

## RF/mmWave structures and process sensitivity

### [Port Metasurface](/work/port-metasurface/)

**Object:** 26 GHz loop–paddle–diamond RF boundary  
**Signal:** CST field response; planned $S_{21}$ bridge  
**Method:** sensing matrix, operator diagnostics, Tikhonov reconstruction  
**Found:** boundary conditions changed hidden-position distinguishability  
**Pending:** fabricated-panel measurement  
**Lesson:** a surface matters only if it changes the operator usefully

## Materials and optical characterization

### [AlN Electroabsorption](/work/aln-electroabsorption/)

**Object:** PEALD-grown AlN-based thin films  
**Signal:** field-dependent UV transmission near 282 nm  
**Method:** structural, electrical, and optical constraints  
**Found:** defect/disorder-mediated absorption was most consistent  
**Failed:** exact defect pathway was not identifiable  
**Lesson:** spectra constrain mechanisms; they do not name microscopic causes alone
