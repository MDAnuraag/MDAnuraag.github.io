My degree is in microelectronics. The projects on this site come from research and from an internship before the fab.

## Day to day

The fab is ramping copy exact, so the process comes from the Korea fab and I'm sorting measurement data from round robin wafers before production. Recipes are still owned in Korea and the local metrology compute isn't fully installed yet, so part of the job is knowing what I can see and what I can't.

Most of it is being comfortable with the tools: how the software handles a number before it reaches me, and whether that number means what it says. I got that from years of poking at my own machines and software.

## Why this site exists

It's a reference for me. I like being able to pull up a piece of what I'm thinking. I use agents to help build and draft it, which is the only reason it gets kept up.

Most of what's here was handed to me, and what I did with it got me to where I am. Some of it failed. I'd rather leave the reasoning visible than clean it up.
